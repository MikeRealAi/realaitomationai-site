London gastropub

Staff onboarding & training platform

The problem. Induction was a ring binder and a conversation. New starters arrived knowing the shift pattern and nothing else, managers chased paperwork for weeks, and nobody could say with confidence who had read the allergen policy.

What we built. A phone-first onboarding flow — contract and e-signature, right-to-work capture, policies with acknowledgement, menu and allergen training, and short knowledge checks — with a manager dashboard showing completion at a glance and prompts for anything outstanding.

What changed. Induction admin went from hours per starter to minutes. New team members arrive having already done the reading, and the compliance record is one search away instead of one filing cabinet away.

Bermuda transport operator

Booking & dispatch platform

The problem. Bookings came in by phone and text, were written down, and were held in one person's head. Drivers found out the day's work by calling in. A busy cruise-ship day was a scramble.

What we built. Public booking pages for each service, a central job board with vehicle and driver assignment, automatic confirmations and reminders to the customer, and a daily schedule each driver can see on their phone.

What changed. Enquiries arrive structured instead of scribbled. The whole day is visible to everyone who needs it, and confirmations go out without anyone picking up the phone.

Events operator

Event enquiry & management app

The problem. Function enquiries arrived across email, a web form and social DMs. Some were answered twice, some not at all, and nobody could say what was in the pipeline for next month.

What we built. A single enquiry pipeline with stages and owners, an intelligent capture widget on the website that asks the qualifying questions before a human gets involved, automatic acknowledgements, and a diary view of everything confirmed.

What changed. Every enquiry has a home and an owner. Response time dropped to minutes, and the pipeline is something you can look at rather than guess at.
