What we build

Six things that give an operator their week back.

Nearly every build is a combination of these. We start with whichever one is costing you the most time, and add the rest when it earns its place.

01 — Onboarding & training

A new starter who's useful on day one.

The offer goes out and the app takes over: contracts signed on a phone, right-to-work captured, policies read and acknowledged, menu and allergen training completed, knowledge checked with short quizzes.

You get a dashboard showing exactly who has completed what, and a record you can hand to an inspector or an employment tribunal without going through a filing cabinet.

Typically includes

  • Digital contracts and e-signature
  • Right-to-work and document capture
  • Policy acknowledgement with audit trail
  • Menu, allergen and service training modules
  • Quizzes, sign-offs and refresher reminders
  • Manager completion dashboard

02 — Rotas & shift management

The week, built in minutes and trusted by everyone.

Draft the rota against your real roles and sections, check it against budgeted hours before you publish, then push it to the team's phones. Swaps, holiday and availability are requested and approved in the app rather than in a group chat at midnight.

Hours come out the other end in the shape your payroll wants, so nobody is retyping a spreadsheet on a Monday morning.

Typically includes

Drag-and-drop rota builder · role and section templates · live labour cost against budget · phone publishing and notifications · swap, holiday and availability requests · clock-in with location or PIN · payroll-ready hours export.

03 — Bookings & enquiry handling

One pipeline, nothing lost at 11pm.

Function enquiries, private dining, events, jobs or appointments — all captured through one form and one pipeline, wherever they come from. Automatic acknowledgements go out instantly, reminders chase the quiet ones, and deposits can be taken without a phone call.

You see every enquiry, its stage, and whose turn it is to move it — instead of three people each assuming someone else replied.

Typically includes

Branded enquiry forms · one pipeline with stages and owners · automatic acknowledgement and follow-up · deposit and payment links · calendar and diary view · contracts and function sheets · reporting on conversion and lost reasons.

04 — Checklists & compliance

Due diligence that exists when you need it.

Opening and closing checks, fridge and probe temperatures, cleaning schedules, deliveries, waste. Completed on a phone, timestamped, with a photo where a photo matters — and flagged to a manager the moment something is out of range or overdue.

When an inspector or an insurer asks, the answer is a search, not an afternoon.

Typically includes

Custom checklist builder · scheduled and recurring tasks · photo and signature capture · temperature logs with out-of-range alerts · overdue escalation to managers · searchable history and exportable records · multi-site view where relevant.

05 — Internal knowledge hub

One version of how you do things.

Dish specs, supplier contacts, recipes and yields, standard operating procedures, allergen matrices, the wine list notes, the thing the old chef knew. Written once, searchable by anyone on shift, updated in one place so the version on the wall and the version on the phone agree.

This is the quiet one nobody asks for and everybody uses by month two.

Typically includes

Searchable article and spec library · recipes with yields and costings · allergen matrix · supplier and contact directory · version history and owner per document · permissions by role · optional AI search so staff can ask a question in plain English.

06 — Dashboards & reporting

The numbers you'd actually change a decision over.

Covers, spend per head, labour percentage, review scores and what the bad ones keep saying, repeat customers, enquiry conversion. Pulled together from the systems you already pay for and shown on one screen, refreshed for you.

Not a data project. Six numbers and the trend, so you walk into Monday knowing where to look.

Typically includes

Connections to your POS, booking and review platforms · a single operator dashboard · weekly summary by email or WhatsApp · review sentiment and theme analysis · labour versus sales tracking · exportable reports for your accountant or owners.

And underneath all of it

The repetitive jobs, running themselves.

Every build comes with automation wired in where it saves real time — not as a separate product you have to buy.

Reminders & follow-ups

Deposit chasers, training due, certificates expiring, bookings tomorrow — sent without anyone remembering.

Data that moves itself

Hours to payroll, enquiries to your diary, sales to your dashboard. Entered once, used everywhere.

Documents generated

Function sheets, contracts, rotas, compliance reports — produced from the data you already captured.

Alerts that matter

A fridge out of range, a check missed, a one-star review landing. Straight to the person who can fix it.
