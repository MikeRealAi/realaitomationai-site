The honest version

You already know where the time goes.

Most independent operators aren't short of software. They're short of one place to look.

  • The rota lives in a spreadsheet that three people edit and nobody fully trusts.
  • New starters get a ring binder, a quick shrug, and a hope that they pick it up.
  • Enquiries land in four places: the inbox, Instagram DMs, the booking widget, and a note behind the bar.
  • The same dozen questions get answered by hand, by you, every single week.
  • Temperature logs, cleaning schedules and allergen sheets live on paper — and vanish when you need them.
  • You pay for three systems and your team genuinely uses one.

None of that needs a bigger team. It needs one place to look, and a handful of jobs that run themselves.

Questions

The things people ask first.

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can run a service, you can run this. We build the app around your words and your process, then train the team in person or on a call. Nobody has to learn an admin panel designed for a software company.

I already pay for a booking system and a payroll tool. Do I have to bin them?

Usually not. Where a tool works and has a way in, we connect to it so your data flows instead of being retyped. We only replace something when keeping it costs you more than it saves.

What does it cost?

Builds start at £450 and are priced as one fixed figure against a written scope — so you know the number before anything begins. What moves the price is how much the app has to do, not how many hours it takes us. Hosting and support are a separate monthly fee you can cancel at any time.

How long does it actually take?

A working first version in two to four weeks for most single-site builds, then a short period of shaping it around real use. Bigger multi-site projects run longer, and we'll say so before you commit.

Who owns the app and the data?

The data is yours, always, and you can export it whenever you want — including on the day you leave. Ownership and licensing of the code is set out in the agreement before we start, so there are no surprises later.

Do you only work with restaurants and pubs?

Hospitality is home, but the same shape of problem turns up in transport, salons, clinics, trades and events — scattered information, repetitive admin, a team on their phones. If that's you, the fit is usually good.

UK or Bermuda?

Both. We're based in London and work across Bermuda, where we have long-standing roots and clients. Most of the work happens remotely, with on-site time where it earns its keep.

Tell me what's eating your week.

Twenty minutes, no pitch deck, no obligation. If I can't help, I'll tell you — and usually point you at who can.
